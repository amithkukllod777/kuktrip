Every stop on the route

Everything a trip needs, nothing it doesn't.

From the first idea to the last receipt — Kuk Trip keeps the plan, the map, the money, and the group in one shared place.

01

Day-by-day itinerary

Drag stops onto a timeline, set times and transport, and watch the day fill in. Reorder anything — the plan updates for everyone.

02

Places on a live map

Search points of interest, pin them, and see the whole route laid out. Distances and clusters make each day easy to read.

03

Budget & expense splitting

Track spend per day, log who paid, and split fairly across the group. Everyone sees exactly what they owe — no spreadsheet.

04

Reservations & docs

Keep flights, hotels, and bookings attached to the right day, with files and confirmation numbers a tap away.

05

Plan as a group

Invite friends or family, leave notes, run a quick poll, and pack together — everyone contributes, nobody gets left out.

06

Works offline

Lost signal in a new city? Your itinerary, map, and lists stay available and sync back up the moment you're online.
