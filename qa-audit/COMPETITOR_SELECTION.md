# Competitor Selection

**Checked:** 2026-07-14 (web + App Store/Play listings + official help docs). Confidence labels per finding.

> Evidence caveat: Wanderlog's and TripIt's marketing pages returned HTTP 403 to automated fetch, so vendor claims are corroborated via App/Play Store listings, official help-desk articles (help.tripit.com), and multiple current (2026) third-party reviews. Claims resting only on third-party reviews are marked PARTIALLY VERIFIED.

## Product framing (Kuk Trip)

- **Category:** Collaborative trip-planning / group-itinerary app (plan-phase, not just booking or journaling).
- **Target users:** Travelers and small groups (friends/family) planning together; privacy-conscious / self-host-inclined users.
- **Core use cases:** Multi-user trip building, day-by-day itinerary, interactive map (Google Places/OSM), budget & per-person cost splitting, packing lists, reservations/bookings, documents, real-time sync, collections, AI trip planner.
- **Business model:** Open-source-derived, self-hostable + likely a hosted tier; privacy positioning ("your data stays yours"). *(INFERRED)*
- **Platforms:** Web (React) primary, PWA/offline; native mobile **NOT VERIFIED**.

## Selected competitors

| # | Competitor | Classification | Why it's the right comparison |
|---|-----------|----------------|-------------------------------|
| 1 | **Wanderlog** | Direct competitor / category leader | Near 1:1 overlap: real-time collaborative editing, map+places, day-by-day itinerary, budget/expense splitting, packing lists, reservation email import, offline, AI planner, public guides. The head-to-head benchmark. |
| 2 | **TripIt (SAP Concur)** | Market leader (adjacent) / close substitute | Dominant itinerary organizer; best-in-class reservation email parsing, flight tracking, calendar sync. Weak on group collaboration — sets the bar for Kuk Trip's reservations/flight features. |
| 3 | **Google Maps / My Maps** | Market leader / free default substitute | The free tool most groups reach for first: shared editable maps, saved lists, Places data, offline maps, unmatched mobile reach. The "good enough & free" gravity every planner must overcome. |

## Considered but rejected

- **Polarsteps** — primarily trip *tracking/journaling*, not collaborative pre-trip planning. Different job-to-be-done. *(PARTIALLY VERIFIED)*
- **Roadtrippers** — narrowly a road-trip/drive-route planner, not a general itinerary+budget+packing planner. *(PARTIALLY VERIFIED)*
- **Layla / Roam Around (AI)** — AI-chat itinerary *generators* with thin collaboration/budgeting/reservations. Worth watching as an emerging AI challenger only.
